# ELECT 275 — Electrical Pipe Bending

Formulas, bender marks, and constants for bending EMT and rigid (RMC) conduit with a hand bender.

[← Back to all classes](../README.md)

---

## Quick reference

- **[Conduit Bending Cheat Sheet (PDF)](conduit_bending_cheat_sheet.pdf)**: the formulas and most important notes from the class on two printable pages:
    + **Formulas:** 90° stub-ups, gain, back-to-back 90s, offsets and shrinkage, 3-point and 4-point saddles, box offsets
    + **Bender shoe marks:** arrow, star, and notch/rim, plus Greenlee and Milwaukee details
    + **Constants:** 90° deductions and gains by conduit size, and offset multipliers and shrink per inch for 10°, 22.5°, 30°, 45° and 60° bends
    + **Practice bends:** layout and shoe alignment for each bend done in class
    + **NEC rules:** the 360° limit between pull points, and support spacing for EMT and RMC

Print it and keep it in your toolbag, or pull it up on your phone at the bending table.

---

## Tips

- **Measure twice, bend once.** Check which mark (arrow, star, or notch) a bend lines up with before you put your foot on the bender.
- **Rigid springs back.** RMC uses the same constants as EMT on the matching shoe, but over-bend by about 2°–3°.
- **Check your angle** with the degree marks on the bender or a torpedo level before you take the conduit out of the shoe.
